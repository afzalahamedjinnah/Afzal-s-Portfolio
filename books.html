Heathens Of Lucifer

Fiction · Dark Psychology · Drama

Published:(Feb 1, 2024)

In the concrete jungles of New York City, a sinister force lurks beneath the surface, orchestrating a symphony of chaos. Unaware to the world, Lucifer himself has taken residence within the unassuming body of a civilian. From childhood, he weaves a web of malevolence, manipulating his host to carry out dark and devious deeds.

As the devil within gains control, a clandestine underworld emerges, ruled by sin and steeped in malevolence. A thriving gangster community forms under the cloak of shadows, with Lucifer at its helm. The City That Never Sleeps becomes a playground for the damned, a stage for the devil to dance upon.

As Lucifer's dark influence spreads, a few courageous individuals emerge to challenge his reign of terror. Will they triumph over the devil in disguise, or will New York succumb to chaos? Brace yourself for a riveting tale of deception, redemption, and the battle for the soul of a city. Will the ordinary prevail against the extraordinary?

Prepare for a descent into the heart of darkness, where the line between good and evil blurs, and the devil himself conducts the orchestra of mayhem.

He, She & The Road

Fiction · Family · Survival

Published:(Nov 17, 2024)

After leaving behind the hustle of Boston, ex-Marine Alvin Axton sets his sights on Valeria City, seeking solace and a fresh start. With his trusted 1965 Chevrolet Camaro roaring to life, he embarks on a soul-searching journey down US Route 78. But the road to redemption is anything but smooth. Along the way, Alvin encounters a series of perplexing puzzles and unexpected twists, each one testing the limits of his military-honed mind and his resolve. As he delves deeper into the mysteries that surround him, Alvin discovers that the true battleground lies within himself. Haunted by his past and driven by an unyielding determination, he navigates the winding highways with a mix of grit and grace. Join Alvin Axton on a gripping ride where every mile brings him closer to unlocking the secrets of his own heart. Will he find the peace he seeks, or will the road ahead only lead to more questions?
