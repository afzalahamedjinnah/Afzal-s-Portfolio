AFZAL AHAMED — PORTFOLIO WEBSITE (REFERENCE DESIGN)

Includes separate pages: Home, Books, Scripts, Blogs, About, Services, Business Presentations, Contact.
Business presentation services are included on the Services page, with company profiles, animated PowerPoint, pitch decks, and redesign.

SETUP:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open VS Code > File > Open Folder > choose this folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open in a browser, or use the Live Server extension.

BEFORE PUBLISHING:
- Replace every your.email@example.com with your real email in contact.html and assets/js/script.js.
- Replace sample book titles, scripts, blog entries, and biography with real information.
- Replace the portrait placeholder with your own photo if desired.
- The remote image URLs need internet access and are placeholders. Use licensed images you have permission to publish.
- Contact form opens the visitor's email application; it does not send/store form data on a server.
- Do not represent sample titles or demo slides as published books or client projects.

PUBLISHING:
Deploy the folder contents through a static host such as Netlify when ready. Review current hosting terms/pricing first.
