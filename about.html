About Me

A writer, dreamer and lifelong learner.

Hi, I'm Afzal Ahamed Jinnah

I'm an operations and marketing professional, published author, and creative storyteller with experience spanning e-commerce, digital content, sales operations, and business coordination.

My professional journey has allowed me to work across different industries and collaborate with teams in operations, marketing, logistics, and customer engagement. I bring together analytical thinking, process coordination, and creative communication to turn complex ideas into clear, practical, and engaging outcomes.

Beyond the corporate environment, I'm passionate about writing novels, developing screenplays, exploring meaningful stories, and creating content that connects with people. My published works, He, She & The Road and Heathens of Lucifer, reflect my interest in storytelling and the different dimensions of human experience.

Through this portfolio, I bring my professional experience and creative interests together — with a focus on purposeful writing, digital content, business communication, and ideas that make an impact. It's also where I begin shaping my long-term vision for Halo Draft, a future media and content solutions venture.

A VISION FOR WHAT'S NEXT

Introducing the Vision Behind

Shaping Ideas. Creating What Comes Next.

Halo Draft is a future-facing vision for a media and content solutions company built around the power of ideas, storytelling and strategic communication.

Envisioned as a creative platform connecting original narratives, screenwriting, digital content and business communication, Halo Draft aspires to bring imagination and purpose together through work that informs, engages and inspires.

The vision is taking shape. The story is only beginning.
